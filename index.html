THE BROWSER AGENT

Say the goal. It fills the form. You approve the submit.

FormPilot reads a page the way a screen reader does, then clicks, types and picks dates step by step while you watch. It asks you for anything missing, hands passwords and card numbers to you, ignores instructions hidden in pages, and always stops for your OK before it books, sends or pays. Try it on five fictional sandbox sites. Demo mode needs no key.
